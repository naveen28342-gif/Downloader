Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download a YouTube video?

Simply paste the YouTube video URL into LinkGrab's input field, click "Fetch Info" to preview the video, choose your preferred quality (up to 4K), and click Download MP4.

Can I download Instagram Reels?

Yes! LinkGrab supports downloading Instagram Reels, Posts, and Stories. Just paste the Instagram link and download in MP4 or MP3 format.

Is LinkGrab free to use?

Yes, LinkGrab is completely free and open source. There are no ads, no tracking, and no hidden fees.

What video qualities are supported?

LinkGrab supports downloading videos in multiple qualities from 144p up to 4K (2160p), depending on the source video's availability. Audio can be extracted at 96, 128, 256, or 320 kbps.

What formats can I download?

Videos are downloaded as MP4 files and audio is extracted as MP3 files — the two most universally compatible formats for any device.
